the concept

Pong,
Evolved.

Two sides. One arena. A projectile bouncing between them — you know the shape.

Volleybolt keeps the volley, then arms each side with a spellbook. Hurl a Fireball to deal escalating damage. Freeze your opponent solid with Frostbolt. Call Thunderstorm to destroy incoming shots. Or Parry at the last second — returning the volley faster than it arrived.

Win rounds by breaking through to the enemy's tower. Lose ground and you fall back — but the losing side draws more mana, keeping every match competitive to the last volley.

how it works
  • Move up and down to position your paddle.
  • Topple the enemy tower to win a round.
  • A tug-of-war — win to advance, lose to fall back.
  • Break through to the enemy's core to win the match.
  • The losing side draws more mana.
  • Towers regrow health over time.
